Our Story

The seed of our coffee journey was planted back in 2017. It was a concept born out of a passion for premium quality and a deep connection to Nepal's majestic Himalayas. The path was not easy; it took us time and extensive effort to understand every aspect of the business. Finally, in 2025, we established our coffee company. Our motive is pure: to offer premium quality coffee, sourced directly from the Himalayas - a product that boasts exceptional taste and benefits your immunity.

Our mission is to take this premium Himalayan coffee to every nook and corner of the world.

From the Hills of Nepal

Nepal's Coffee Belt

Nepal's coffee is grown at elevations of 1,000–2,000m in the lush hillside districts. Our beans are sourced directly from these renowned growing regions, ensuring the finest quality in every cup.

☕ Nepal Coffee Growing Districts

🌿

Gulmi

Altitude: 1,200–2,000m

The heartland of Nepali coffee. Gulmi's misty hills and rich soil produce beans with a naturally sweet, fruity profile — the pride of Nepal's specialty coffee scene.

Single OriginHigh Altitude
⛰️

Palpa

Altitude: 1,000–1,700m

Palpa's terraced hillsides yield a well-balanced cup with notes of honey and dark chocolate. One of Nepal's oldest and most respected coffee-growing regions.

Honey ProcessHeirloom Varieties
🏔️

Syangja

Altitude: 1,100–1,900m

Nestled between dramatic ridgelines, Syangja coffee carries a bright acidity and floral aroma — ideal for light to medium roasts that reveal Nepal's terroir.

Washed ProcessFloral Notes
🌄

Kaski (Pokhara)

Altitude: 1,000–2,100m

With the Annapurna range as its backdrop, Kaski produces some of Nepal's most aromatic beans — a silky body with subtle caramel and walnut undertones.

AromaticPremium Grade
🌱

Arghakhanchi

Altitude: 800–1,600m

An emerging coffee district gaining recognition for its natural-process beans. Rich, wine-like complexity makes Arghakhanchi coffee a specialty collector's favourite.
